About me

Hi, I'm Johnny. I earned a B.S. in Cognitive Science with a minor in Computer Science from UC San Diego, where I studied interaction design and how people learn and use technology. I'm a developer by hobby and keep learning web development by building small, useful tools.

By day I support students in special education with the San Diego County Office of Education. That work shapes how I build: I care about clear interfaces, accessible formats, and software that saves educators time.
